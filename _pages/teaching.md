# Teaching Philosophy

I believe teaching is most effective when students actively engage with the material and connect theoretical concepts to real-world problems. My approach emphasizes:

- **Hands-on learning** through practical coding sessions and data analysis
- **Critical thinking** by encouraging students to question assumptions and interpret results carefully
- **Inclusive education**, ensuring that students from diverse backgrounds feel supported and valued

I strive to foster curiosity, independence, and practical skills—particularly in **computational modeling, statistics, and artificial intelligence**. My goal is to help students confidently apply learned methods to real datasets and interpret their findings responsibly and ethically.
